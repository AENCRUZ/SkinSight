For now

Avoid Touching

Try not to scratch or pick at the affected area.

Avoid New Products

Hold off on new soaps, lotions, or cosmetics on the area.

Keep the Area Clean

Gently wash with mild soap and water, and pat dry.

Monitor Changes

Note any change in size, color, or new symptoms.

These are general self-care suggestions and are not a diagnosis or prescription. If symptoms are severe or getting worse quickly, seek urgent medical care.
