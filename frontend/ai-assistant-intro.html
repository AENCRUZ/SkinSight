What to expect

Three simple steps

Each conversation builds toward a clear summary you can bring to your appointment.

Describe your concern

Answer a short set of questions about symptoms, how long you've had them, where they are and what might trigger them.

Add a photo (optional)

Attach or take a clear photo of the visible skin concern right inside the conversation for general, non-diagnostic observations.

Review your summary

Get a structured consultation summary to download, copy or take with you to your dermatologist.

Before you begin

SKINSIGHT helps organize information for your dermatologist. It does not diagnose conditions or prescribe medication. If your symptoms are severe or getting worse quickly, seek urgent medical care instead.

Your messages and any photo you attach are sent to an AI service to generate replies. Consultations from this browser session stay in this tab only and are cleared when you close it or sign out.
