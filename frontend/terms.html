1. What SKINSIGHT is

SKINSIGHT is a tool that helps you organize what you notice about your skin, prepare a summary to bring to a dermatologist, find listed clinics, and keep a personal medication list. By creating an account or using the service you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use it.

2. Not medical advice

  • SKINSIGHT does not diagnose any condition, does not prescribe or recommend medication, and does not replace a dermatologist or other health professional.
  • Nothing in the app, including assistant replies, summaries and general information, is a medical opinion or a promise about your health.
  • In an emergency (for example trouble breathing, swelling of the face, lips or throat, or a fast-spreading rash with fever) contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for SKINSIGHT.
  • Always ask a qualified professional before changing, starting or stopping any treatment.

3. The AI assistant

  • The assistant is an AI. It can misunderstand you, leave things out or make mistakes. Check anything important with a professional.
  • It is designed to ask questions, record what you report and share general skin-health information. It is not designed to name conditions or recommend medicines. It may still occasionally say something inappropriate, and you should not rely on it if it does.
  • The consultation summary is based on what you told the assistant. Please read it and correct anything wrong before sharing it. You are responsible for what you share with others.
  • Messages and any photo you attach are processed by third-party AI and hosting services as described in the Privacy Policy.

4. Your account

  • Tell the project team if you think someone else has accessed your account.
  • Use only your own account.

6. Clinic directory and contacting clinics

  • The directory is a demonstration that currently lists clinics in Batangas Province, Philippines only.
  • Details such as hours, phone numbers, emails, doctors and services have not been independently verified and may be wrong or out of date. Contact the clinic and check its credentials before you rely on anything.
  • Open or closed labels are based on the hours listed in the directory.
  • SKINSIGHT does not book appointments, send messages for you, or act as a go-between. The email draft only opens in your own email service. What you send to a clinic is your decision, and the clinic's handling of it is its own.
  • Google Maps and other third-party links lead to services we do not control.

7. Medication tracker

The tracker is a personal notebook stored in your browser on this device. It sends no reminders or notifications unless you turn on dose reminders, which are best-effort: a notification can arrive late or not at all (for example if your device is offline, your browser is closed in a way that stops background activity, or notifications are blocked), so never rely on them alone. The tracker cannot tell whether you actually took a dose, and gives no medical advice. Data can be lost if you clear your browser data, and it is not backed up. Follow your doctor's and pharmacist's instructions, not the tracker.
