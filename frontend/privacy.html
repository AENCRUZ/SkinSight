1. What SKINSIGHT is

SKINSIGHT helps you write down a skin concern, prepare a summary for a dermatologist, find listed clinics and keep a personal medication list. It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe. See the Terms of Use.

3. How the AI assistant uses your information

  • Your messages (and photo, if any) are processed by Google's Gemini to produce a reply or a summary. To keep a conversation coherent, the earlier messages of the open consultation are sent again with each new message.
  • The assistant is instructed to document what you report, not to name possible conditions and not to recommend medicines. An automatic check on its replies is a second safeguard, but it cannot guarantee that every reply is appropriate.
  • The consultation summary is written from what you said. It is shown to you, can be downloaded or copied by you, and is stored with the consultation.
  • We have not confirmed how Google keeps or uses data sent to Gemini under the account this project uses (for example, whether it can be used to improve Google's products). That depends on the terms and plan of that account. Until that is confirmed, assume anything you send to the assistant may be processed under Google's terms, and do not send anything you would not want a third-party AI provider to handle.

6. Cookies and tracking

SKINSIGHT's own code does not set cookies, and we found no analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app. Some services listed above (for example Google Maps and Google sign-in) may set their own cookies, which SKINSIGHT does not control.

7. How long information is kept

  • Saved consultations stay in your account until you delete them. There is no automatic deletion yet.
  • Browser session data ends when you close the tab or sign out.
  • Medication data stays on your device until you delete it or clear your browser data.
  • Dose reminder data stays on the SKINSIGHT server until you turn reminders off, or until your browser's push service reports the subscription as expired.
  • Data held by Google (Gemini), Render, Supabase backups, and the other services follows their own rules. We have not confirmed their retention periods, so we do not state one.

8. Your choices

  • Delete a saved consultation: use the bin button next to it in the AI assistant's history panel. This removes it from the database.
  • Delete medication data: use "Delete medication data on this device" on the medication pages.
  • Stop dose reminders: press "Turn off reminders" on the medication tracker. This deletes the copy on the server.
  • Choose what you share: a photo and your location are optional. The browser asks before sharing your position, and you can refuse.
  • Sign out to clear this session from the browser.
  • Delete your whole account: there is no self-service option yet (see "Things we have not settled yet").

9. Security

  • Your browser connects to the SKINSIGHT server, Supabase and the other services over HTTPS, which encrypts data in transit.
  • Database rules stop other signed-in users from reading your saved consultations, and the AI service key is kept on the server, not in the web pages.
  • We have not verified how the hosting providers protect stored data, so we make no claim about encryption of stored data. No online service is perfectly secure.

10. Health information and photos

What you tell the assistant about your skin is health-related and can be sensitive. Share only what you are comfortable sharing. Avoid including names, ID numbers, addresses or other people's details in messages, and avoid photos that show your face or identifying marks unless that is necessary. Only upload photos of yourself, or of someone who has agreed.

If you have trouble breathing, swelling of the face or lips, or a rapidly spreading rash with fever, contact emergency services. Do not rely on SKINSIGHT.
