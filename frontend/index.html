AI Skin Assistant

Talk it through. Leave with a summary.

The assistant asks one question at a time: symptoms, duration, location and recent changes. Add a photo if it helps, and it describes what's visible in general, non-diagnostic terms. When you're ready, generate a summary to download or copy for your appointment.

  • Plain-language questions, one at a time
  • Earlier consultations saved to your account

    Tell Us Your Concern

    Add a Photo

  4. 04

Our Approach

Designed to Support, Not Replace, Dermatologists

SKINSIGHT provides general information, consultation preparation, and organizational support. It does not provide a definitive diagnosis or prescribe medication.
