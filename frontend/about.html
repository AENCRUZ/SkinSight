About SKINSIGHT

SKINSIGHT is an AI-assisted platform for organizing skin concerns and preparing for professional dermatology care.

What we do

What SKINSIGHT Does

Understand

Organize what you're noticing about a skin concern into clear, useful information.

Prepare

Build a consultation summary so your dermatologist visit starts with the full picture.

Connect

Find nearby dermatologists and clinics, and share your summary when you reach out.

Manage

Track prescribed skin medications, schedules, and doses over time.

Safety First

SKINSIGHT does not provide definitive diagnoses, does not prescribe medication, and does not replace a dermatologist. It is designed to support — not substitute — professional dermatology care, by helping you organize information and prepare for your visit.
